SeniorFindr Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 8, 2026

1. Introduction

SeniorFindr ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide:

  • Account information (name, email, password)
  • Profile information (age, location, interests, lifestyle preferences)
  • Listing information (property details, photos, descriptions)
  • Messages and communications with other users
  • Interview notes and responses

Information Collected Automatically:

  • Location data (when you grant permission) to show nearby listings
  • Device information and usage data
  • Photos (when you upload listing or profile images)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our services
  • To match you with compatible roommates using our compatibility system
  • To enable communication between users
  • To verify addresses and show nearby listings
  • To send notifications about matches, messages, and updates
  • To prevent fraud and ensure platform safety

4. Information Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal information. We may share information:

  • With other users you choose to contact or match with
  • With family members you explicitly invite to your Family Portal
  • With service providers (Firebase, Google) who help us operate the app
  • When required by law or to protect safety

5. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud) with industry-standard encryption. We retain your data as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Update or correct your information
  • Delete your account and associated data at any time from the Profile & Settings screen in the app
  • Opt-out of non-essential communications
  • Control location and photo permissions

7. Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly in the app: open Profile & Settings, then tap Delete Account. This removes your profile, listings, and authentication account. You may also request deletion by emailing us at the address below.

8. Location Data

We use your location to:

  • Show nearby housing listings
  • Help verify addresses when creating listings
  • Calculate distances to amenities

You can disable location access in your device settings, though some features may be limited.

9. Photos and Camera

We access your photo library and camera only when you choose to:

  • Upload a profile picture
  • Add photos to a listing
  • Share images in messages

10. Children's Privacy

SeniorFindr is intended for users aged 50+. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

11. Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data, including encryption, secure authentication, and access controls. However, no system is 100% secure.

12. Third-Party Services

We use:

  • Firebase (Google) for authentication, database, and storage
  • Google Sign-In for authentication

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify you of significant changes via email or app notification.

14. Contact Us

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact us at:

Email: seniorfindr@gmail.com

15. California Privacy Rights

California residents have additional rights under the CCPA, including the right to know what information we collect and the right to deletion.

16. Consent

By using SeniorFindr, you consent to this Privacy Policy.
